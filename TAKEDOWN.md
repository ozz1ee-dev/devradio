# Takedown procedure

We take rights complaints seriously. This procedure covers tracks submitted to this repository and
broadcast on the station. It is a practical policy, not legal advice.

## How to report

Contact the maintainers by either route:

1. **Email:** `takedown@devradio.co.uk`.
2. **GitHub issue:** title it `[TAKEDOWN] <track id or URL>` and include the details below.
   For sensitive personal information, email instead of opening a public issue.

Please include:

- Your name, organisation (if any), and contact details.
- The track ID, repository path, or AzuraCast/station URL at issue.
- The specific rights you own or control, and why the submission is unauthorised.
- The licence you believe applies, if any, and any contrary evidence.
- A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorised.
- A statement that the information is accurate and that you are the rights holder or authorised to
  act for them.
- Your signature (typed is acceptable).

## What happens next

| Step | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Acknowledge receipt | 2 business days |
| Precautionary disable in AzuraCast (track off air while checked) | 48 hours of receipt (proposal) |
| Verify the claim and identify affected files | 2 business days |
| Remove from the AzuraCast playlist and repository | Within 5 business days of a verified claim |
| Notify the contributor | After removal |
| Resolve attribution/licence error, if applicable | Within 7 business days of the proposal |

Urgent cases (for example, a credible claim involving unreleased or personal material) may be
removed immediately, before verification is complete.

## Removal and proposal

On a verified claim, maintainers will:

1. Remove the track from active AzuraCast playlists and, where possible, from the media library.
2. Remove the `tracks/<slug>/` folder (or mark it disabled) from the repository.
3. Record the takedown in the moderation log, including the track ID and date.
4. Tell the contributor what was removed and why, without disclosing more personal data than
   necessary.

**Remediation proposal.** The rights holder may propose a narrower fix, such as corrected
attribution or a different licence, instead of permanent removal. The contributor may respond with
evidence of their licence. Maintainers decide within 7 business days. A rejected proposal does not
delay removal; the track stays down while the dispute is reviewed.

## Counter-notice

If you submitted the track and believe the takedown was mistaken, reply to the removal notice with:

- The track ID and the date of removal.
- Your contact details.
- An explanation of why you have the right to submit and broadcast the track.
- Supporting evidence (licence, generation record, plan, permission from the rights holder).
- A statement that you consent to the information being shared with the complainant as needed.

Maintainers may restore a track only after reviewing the counter-notice and, where appropriate,
contacting the complainant. We are not obliged to restore content while a good-faith dispute
remains unresolved.

## Repeat infringers

Accounts that repeatedly submit unauthorised tracks, or that misrepresent licence attestations,
may be blocked from contributing. Repeated or deliberate infringement may be reported to GitHub
and, where required, to the relevant rights holder.

## Bad-faith claims

Knowingly false or abusive takedown claims may be rejected and reported. We keep a record of all
notices so that patterns can be reviewed.

## Scope

This procedure applies to audio, metadata, artwork, and comments in this repository and to the
station's AzuraCast media library. It does not replace any statutory takedown process available in
your jurisdiction.
